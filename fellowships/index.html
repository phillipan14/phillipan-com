Fellowships

A directory of fellowships

I believe opportunity should be democratized. Most of these programs were invisible to me when I was younger — I learned about them through friends, mentors, and luck. The information shouldn’t have been that hard to find.

So this is the directory I wish I’d had: a categorized, filterable, searchable map of fellowships, scholarships, leadership programs, and prizes across foreign policy, tech, US–China, transatlantic, leadership, research, climate, social impact, and graduate lanes. Most are free. Most have nomination paths. Many are open globally.

If you’re building your own pipeline — or helping someone else build theirs — start here.

Last updated June 2026. Deadlines drift; confirm on the program page before applying. Got one I missed? Tell me.
