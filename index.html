I'm a founder and operator.

I run Allston Labs, where I'm currently building Skylarq — the operating system for outbound, helping founders and operators run high-quality cold outreach at scale. Allston is also home to a small set of consumer products: Echo, Rosetta, and Nuance.

Previously, I co-founded Homebase, the first Vietnamese company backed by Y Combinator (W21), where we raised $30M to make homeownership accessible across Southeast Asia.

Separately from Allston, I'm passionate about education at scale. I'm building an AI-native school that explores what apprenticeship-style learning looks like when an AI tutor is part of the cohort. I work with the International School of Beijing on integrating AI into K–12 classrooms, and previously taught at Bodley Academy in Hong Kong. This thread of work is supported in part by the Chang Fellowship. On the personal side, I'm writing To My Future Kids — a long-arc letter project that will become a book.

Before Homebase I worked at Goldman Sachs and McKinsey. I studied computer science at Caltech, earned an MS/MBA at Harvard (the School of Engineering & Applied Sciences and Harvard Business School), and a Master's at Tsinghua University in Beijing as a Schwarzman Scholar.
