I'm a founder and operator.

I'm currently building four things in parallel: Allston Labs (forward-deployed AI engineering for go-to-market), Quartz Labs (the data layer for frontier AI), Arena School (an AI-native school), and Forward Indicators (a research lab that reads China as an engineered system).

Previously, I co-founded Homebase — the first Vietnamese company backed by Y Combinator (W21), where we raised $30M to make homeownership accessible across Southeast Asia.

I'm also writing To My Future Kids — a long-arc letter project that will become a book — and shipping a few small consumer apps on the side.

Before Homebase I worked at Goldman Sachs and McKinsey. I studied computer science at Caltech, earned an MS/MBA at Harvard (the School of Engineering & Applied Sciences and Harvard Business School), and a Master's at Tsinghua University in Beijing as a Schwarzman Scholar.

Now

  • Allston Labs — forward-deployed AI engineering for go-to-market. We embed AI engineers in our customers' Slack to build and run custom sales workflows in production. We don't sell software — we own the outcome. allstonlabs.com
  • Quartz Labs — the data layer for frontier AI. We source, annotate, and ship training data for frontier AI labs across voice, robotics, expert evaluation, and multimodal. quartzlabs.co
  • Arena School — an AI-native school for what AI can't teach: agency, taste, craft, courage, judgment. First pilot runs at the International School of Beijing in August 2026. arenaschool.org
  • Forward Indicators — a public research lab that reads China as an engineered system. LLM-powered forecasting pipelines on Chinese AI and tech policy, with Brier-scored predictions. First three projects ship between July and December 2026. forwardindicators.com
  • To My Future Kids — a long-arc letter project becoming a book. Lives at tomyfuture.kids.
  • Side projects — Rosetta and Nuance are live on the App Store; Echo is on TestFlight. A few more in progress.
