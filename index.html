I'm a founder and operator.

I'm building Skylarq — the operating system for outbound, helping founders and operators run high-quality cold outreach at scale. Previously, I co-founded Homebase, the first Vietnamese company backed by Y Combinator (W21), where we raised $30M to make homeownership accessible across Southeast Asia.

Before Homebase I worked at Goldman Sachs and McKinsey. I studied computer science at Caltech and earned an MBA at Harvard Business School.

I also run Allston Labs, a workshop for small consumer products — an iOS recorder called Echo, a translation utility called Rosetta, a keyboard called Nuance, and an AI-native school called Arena.

Now

  • Building Skylarq — outbound-as-a-service for founders and operators. We run high-quality outbound campaigns end-to-end so founders can spend their time on the meetings that matter.
  • Allston Labs — my workshop for small consumer products. Echo, Rosetta, Nuance, and Arena are all live or in-progress.
