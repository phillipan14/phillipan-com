I'm a founder and operator.

I run Allston Labs, where I'm currently building Skylarq — the operating system for outbound, helping founders and operators run high-quality cold outreach at scale. Allston is also home to a small set of consumer products: Echo, Rosetta, Nuance, Arena, and a few experiments in progress.

Previously, I co-founded Homebase, the first Vietnamese company backed by Y Combinator (W21), where we raised $30M to make homeownership accessible across Southeast Asia.

Before Homebase I worked at Goldman Sachs and McKinsey. I studied computer science at Caltech and earned an MBA at Harvard Business School.

Now

  • Skylarq — outbound-as-a-service for founders and operators. We run high-quality outbound campaigns end-to-end so founders can spend their time on the meetings that matter. skylarq.ai
  • Consumer products — Rosetta is live on the App Store; Echo, Nuance, and Arena are in active development. See projects.
