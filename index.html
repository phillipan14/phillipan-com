Phillip Guangning An

I'm a founder, operator, and a current Schwarzman Scholar at Tsinghua University in Beijing.

I'm building Skylarq — the operating system for outbound, helping founders and operators run high-quality cold outreach at scale. Previously, I co-founded Homebase, the first Vietnamese company backed by Y Combinator (W21), where we raised $30M to make homeownership accessible across Southeast Asia.

I also build small things under Allston Labs — an iOS notes app called Echo, a translation utility called Rosetta, a keyboard called Nuance, and an AI-native school called Arena.
